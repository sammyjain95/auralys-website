Secure AI Programme: own your AI stack.

The idea, in one line: set up your own private, secure AI, on a secure cloud or on-premise, so your data never leaves your control.

Why now

Public AI tools are useful, but your prompts and documents pass through someone else's system. For defence, banking, law, pharma, and manufacturing research, that is a risk. Owning your stack protects your data, and it protects your work if export restrictions or access limits change.

A secure cloud costs far less than on-premise hardware while keeping the same level of data privacy, when it is set up correctly. That is the core of this programme: the same control, without the hardware bill.

Two routes, your choice

Recommended for most teamsSecure cloud

Your private cloud account, closed network, encryption, your access rules. No hardware to buy. Faster to start.

Where it fitsOn-premise

Your own servers, for offline work or where policy requires it. Full control, higher upfront cost and maintenance.

We help you choose after discovery, based on your policy, data, and team.

What the programme covers

  1. Discovery and data audit. How you use AI today, what must stay private, and where the risk sits. No change in this step, a clear picture first.
  2. Design your secure setup. Private environment, access rules, encryption, and logging, approved by you before we build.
  3. Build, and train your team. Setup inside your environment, plus practical training so your team uses it safely, day to day.
  4. Train your custom models, and hand over. Models trained on your own data, inside your environment. Full control and documentation handed to your team.

What you leave with

  • A private AI environment you own.
  • Clear access rules and logs you can show an auditor.
  • A trained team.
  • Custom models trained on your data, that stay in your control.

Who it is for

Technology, data, and AI leaders, and heads of business units, in regulated and data-sensitive companies in India and internationally. Defence and aerospace, banking and insurance, law, pharma, manufacturing research, and other IP-heavy teams.
