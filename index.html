From the founder

AI should remove busywork, not judgment.

Most businesses do not have a data problem. They have a translation problem. The numbers exist, but the story does not, and by the time the deck is built, the decision window has already closed.

People should spend their hours on judgment, not busywork. AI is at its best when it does the tedious work and hands the thinking back to people.

I started Auralys AI on a simple belief: AI should earn its place in a business by moving a number that matters. So we deploy agentic AI employees, data strategies, and web platforms that deliver measurable outcomes, and we build products like the AI Analyst to prove we can ship.

On this page I share what I am learning as we deploy AI inside real companies: what works, what fails, and the unglamorous process fixes that matter more than the models. No hype, just field notes.

Sumant Jain
Founder, Auralys AI

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does an AI consultant do?
An AI consultant helps you find where AI creates measurable business value, then designs and deploys it. At Auralys AI that means agentic AI employees such as voice assistants and workflow automation, strategic data consulting, and the web platforms to run them on.
How does Auralys AI engage with clients?
Three models: advisory retainers for ongoing AI strategy guidance, fixed-scope project builds with agreed deliverables and timelines, and product accelerators that move from idea to working software fast. You work directly with the founder in every model.
How long does a typical AI engagement take?
Discovery and roadmapping typically run two to four weeks. Fixed-scope builds usually ship in four to twelve weeks depending on complexity. Timelines are agreed in writing before work starts, so there are no surprises.
How is pricing structured?
Fixed-scope engagements carry a fixed price agreed before work begins, so you know the cost up front. Advisory work runs on a monthly retainer. There is no hourly billing and no surprise invoices.
How does Auralys AI handle data security?
Auralys AI uses bank-grade encryption and follows enterprise compliance standards. Client data is handled under strict confidentiality agreements and is never shared or repurposed without consent.
Which countries does Auralys AI serve?
Auralys AI serves clients across India (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Kolkata), South Africa (Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria), the United Kingdom (London, Manchester, Edinburgh), and the United States.
