Questions, answered

Things people ask before signing up.

Do I need to share my account passwords?
No. Nothing is shared on stage. We use a fictional sample household (Bob and Linda) for the demo. Your real numbers stay yours.
Is this a sales pitch?
No. About 70% of the session is teaching: a framework you can use with any tool, even a notebook. We do mention Ember and offer a waitlist spot at the end, but there's no pricing talk and no pressure.
I'm not tech-savvy. Will I keep up?
Yes. Big fonts, slow clicks, every move narrated in plain words. If you can read a bank statement, you can follow along. A recording is sent afterward so you can rewatch.
What if I can't make the live time?
Register anyway. You'll get the recording plus the printable checklist the next day.
Is my data safe with Ember?
Ember is read-only: it shows your money, it never moves it. Bank-grade encryption, and you can disconnect any account at any time.
