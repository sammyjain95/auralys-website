AI Business Analyst

Launch App
What is Auralys AI's AI Business Analyst tool?+
The AI Business Analyst is Auralys AI's flagship product. It converts raw business data — spreadsheets, CSVs, or pasted figures — into a polished, board-ready PowerPoint presentation. It identifies trends, anomalies, and strategic signals automatically, then structures them into an executive-ready narrative, typically in minutes rather than the hours a manual analyst review would take.
What file formats can I upload?+
You can upload Excel files, CSV files, or paste raw numbers directly into the tool. It works with sales figures, financial statements, survey data, and operational metrics in most common formats.
Is my data stored or used to train AI models?+
No. Uploaded files are processed in-session only to generate the presentation you requested. Files are not retained on Auralys AI's servers after the session ends, and your data is never used to train AI models or shared with third parties beyond what is necessary to run the tool.
How long does it take to generate a presentation?+
Most decks are generated in minutes. You upload your data, set a brief describing your audience and the narrative you need, and the AI Business Analyst handles structuring and formatting automatically.
Can I customize the output with my own branding?+
Yes. The tool produces a polished PowerPoint file that you can further customize with your own branding, colors, and logo before presenting it to your board, investors, or department heads.
Is there a free trial?+
Yes. Auralys AI offers your first deck for free so you can evaluate the tool's output quality before committing.

Website Development

What kind of websites does Auralys build?+
Auralys AI builds professional, conversion-focused websites for businesses, including informational and corporate sites, B2B product catalogues, and full e-commerce platforms — from product listing through checkout. The focus is on UX, design quality, and measurable conversion outcomes rather than templated builds.
Do you build e-commerce sites, or just informational sites?+
Both. Auralys AI delivers everything from brochure-style informational and portfolio sites to complete e-commerce platforms with product catalogues, cart, checkout, and payment integration, depending on what a client's business model requires.
How long does a typical website project take?+
Timelines depend on scope. A focused informational or catalogue site can typically be delivered in a few weeks, while a full e-commerce build with custom integrations takes longer. Auralys AI scopes each project individually after an initial consultation.
Do you work with businesses outside India?+
Yes. Auralys AI has existing clients across India, South Africa, and the United Kingdom, and builds websites calibrated to the regulatory, payment, and commercial conventions of each market.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance after launch?+
Yes. Auralys AI offers ongoing support and maintenance arrangements after launch, covering updates, fixes, and incremental improvements. Specific terms are agreed with each client based on project scope.
What's the typical cost range for a website project?+
Cost depends on scope, complexity, and market. Auralys AI provides a tailored quote after an initial consultation rather than a fixed price list, since informational sites, B2B catalogues, and full e-commerce builds carry very different scopes of work.

Strategic Data Consulting

What does strategic data consulting with Auralys involve?+
Strategic data consulting is for organizations whose challenges don't fit a packaged product. Auralys AI's team works directly with a client's business to identify where AI and data can have the highest impact, then designs and delivers a solution tailored to that specific challenge.
Do I need to already have AI infrastructure in place?+
No. Auralys AI works with organizations at any stage of AI maturity, including those with no existing data infrastructure. The engagement typically begins with an assessment of current systems and processes before any solution is designed.
What industries do you typically work with?+
Auralys AI works across finance, marketing, and operations functions for businesses in India, South Africa, and the UK, with particular depth in SME and growing enterprise contexts where off-the-shelf tools don't fit the business model.
How is consulting engagement priced — project-based or retainer?+
Both models are available. Some engagements are scoped as a fixed project with defined deliverables, while others run as an ongoing retainer for continued strategic input. The right structure depends on the nature of the problem being solved.
Can consulting lead into a custom-built tool, like the AI Analyst?+
Yes. Several Auralys AI products, including the AI Business Analyst, originated from solving a specific client problem during a consulting engagement. Where a recurring need justifies it, consulting work can evolve into a dedicated tool.
